协同边缘计算作为一种新兴范式，通过利用地理分布且异构的边缘节点资源，为自动驾驶、工业互联网等低延迟、高可靠应用提供了技术支撑。然而，边缘环境的异构性、计算与网络资源的紧密耦合性，以及分布式智能应用所呈现的复杂网络拓扑，使得传统的任务调度机制在资源效率和系统性能上面临严峻挑战。当前研究主要存在两大局限：其一，现有工作大多关注任务间具有静态依赖关系的调度问题，难以有效刻画分布式训练中可能存在的环状等复杂通信拓扑，且普遍将计算资源与网络资源进行独立管理与调度，忽略了二者间的内在权衡；其二，在多作业并发场景下，现有调度方案缺乏对计算与网络资源的联合感知与协同分配，容易引发资源竞争、作业饥饿及系统整体性能下降。为解决上述问题，本文在协同边缘计算环境下设计了一种高效的计算与网络资源联合调度方案，主要工作如下：

本文设计并实现了协同边缘计算任务调度系统CES（Collaborative Edge Scheduler），将任务映射与带宽分配进行联合管理，并构建了计算与网络资源的联合优化模型，以精确描述分布式训练等任务的通信拓扑与资源需求。在此基础上，提出基于深度强化学习（DRL，Deep Reinforcement Learning）的CES-PPO算法，采用双分支演员-评论家架构与启发式奖励函数，可在动态环境中自主学习近似全局最优调度策略。仿真与系统实验表明，该算法整体性能显著优于现有基准方法。

针对多分布式学习作业并发场景，进一步提出CES-Multi-Jobs算法。该算法以提升资源均衡度、保障带宽需求满足度及降低平均等待时间目标，结合滚动窗口、资源感知贪心排序与动态饥饿保护策略，在多资源约束下实现高效调度。实验表明，该方法在资源利用率、作业等待时间与带宽满足度方面均优于现有方法，增强了CES系统在多作业场景中的适用性与综合性能。

\chapter{Abstract}
Collaborative edge computing, as an emerging paradigm, provides technical support for low‑latency and high‑reliability applications such as autonomous driving and Industrial Internet of Things by leveraging geographically distributed and heterogeneous edge node resources. However, the heterogeneity of edge environments, the tight coupling between computing and network resources, and the complex network topologies exhibited by distributed intelligent applications pose severe challenges to traditional task scheduling mechanisms in terms of resource efficiency and system performance. Current research suffers from two major limitations. First, most existing work focuses on scheduling problems with static dependencies among tasks, which cannot effectively capture the cyclic or other complex communication topologies that may exist in distributed training. Moreover, computing and network resources are generally managed and scheduled independently, ignoring the inherent trade‑offs between them. Second, in multi‑job concurrent scenarios, existing scheduling schemes lack joint awareness and coordinated allocation of computing and network resources, which easily leads to resource contention, job starvation, and degradation of overall system performance. To address these issues, this paper designs an efficient joint scheduling scheme for computing and network resources in collaborative edge computing environments. The main contributions are as follows.

This paper designs and implements a Collaborative Edge Scheduler (CES), which jointly manages task-to-node mapping and bandwidth allocation, and constructs a joint optimization model of computational and network resources to accurately describe the communication topology and resource requirements of tasks such as distributed training. On this basis, a deep reinforcement learning (DRL) based algorithm, CES-PPO, is proposed. It adopts a dual-branch actor-critic architecture and a heuristic reward function, enabling the system to autonomously learn a near-globally optimal scheduling policy in dynamic environments. Simulation and system experiments show that the proposed algorithm significantly outperforms existing baseline methods in overall performance.

For scenarios with multiple concurrent distributed learning jobs, the CES-Multi-Jobs algorithm is further proposed. Aiming to improve resource utilization balance, guarantee bandwidth demand satisfaction, and reduce average job waiting time, this algorithm incorporates a rolling window mechanism, resource-aware greedy sorting, and a dynamic starvation protection strategy under multi-resource constraints on edge nodes. Experimental results demonstrate that CES-Multi-Jobs achieves superior performance in resource utilization, job waiting time, and bandwidth satisfaction compared to existing methods, thereby enhancing the applicability and overall performance of the CES system in multi-job scheduling scenarios.
